Non-negotiable boundaries

AUTHORIZATION ≠ EXECUTIONPermission never proves that the external action happened.
EXECUTION ≠ OBSERVATIONThe worker's response is not independent evidence.
OBSERVATION ≠ VERIFICATIONEvidence must still be compared with the authorized effect.
