# Spiral Wealth — Commercial Thesis

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Status:** Commercial thesis approved for execution; market validation pending.

## 1. Company

**Spiral Wealth = Governed Execution Infrastructure.**

The economic capability is **Independent Outcome Verification (IOV)**: independently observing external state and reconciling it against an authorized intended effect.

The customer does not buy queues, databases, cryptography, workflow automation, or generic observability. The customer buys reduced exposure to **blind execution risk** when software, automations, and AI agents change state outside the company's direct control.

> Govern execution. Verify outcomes.

## 2. Causal economic chain

```text
AUTOMATION / AGENT
  -> EXECUTION ACCEPTED
  -> EXTERNAL STATE
  -> INDEPENDENT OBSERVATION
  -> RECONCILIATION
  -> VERDICT
  -> VERIFIABLE RECEIPT
```

The critical distinction is between an executor reporting what it attempted and an independent verifier determining whether the authorized effect was actually observed.

## 3. Commercial wedge

**The 14-Day Verification Sprint**

- One critical outbound workflow.
- One external provider/system.
- Lightweight integration; customer keeps its existing executor.
- 14 days of controlled observation and reconciliation.
- First real receipt as the physical proof point.
- Executive report showing detected divergences and operational implications.
- Pilot target price: **R$10,000**, convertible into an annual contract.

The adoption principle is: **invisible on entry, implacable on exit.** The customer should not have to replace its execution engine to obtain independent verification.

## 5. Economic buyer and value

The value proposition is not "more logs." Existing IAM, SIEM, API gateways, Sentry, Datadog, New Relic, workflow engines, and durable execution systems may establish internal execution evidence, but Spiral's thesis is to add an independent observation/reconciliation layer for external outcomes.

The customer can answer:

> Did the external state actually correspond to what our policy authorized?

This is a different question from:

> Did our service receive HTTP 200?

## 8. Competitive thesis

Spiral should not claim that competitors are universally incapable of verification. The precise thesis is narrower:

- Observability tools primarily expose internal telemetry and service health.
- Durable workflow engines primarily make execution resilient and resumable.
- Automation/iPaaS platforms primarily coordinate integrations and workflows.
- IAM/policy systems authorize access/actions.

Spiral specializes in the boundary between **authorized intended effect** and **observed external state**, using separated execution, observation, and verification responsibilities.

The moat hypothesis is cumulative: provider adapters, observation semantics, reconciliation rules, failure knowledge, and integration into operational governance.
