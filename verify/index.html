Receipt verification

This endpoint is intentionally explicit about what is and is not being verified.

Verification requires the receipt, its signature, and the verifier public key.

The public site does not manufacture or substitute evidence. A receipt can be verified independently with a standard Ed25519 implementation (for example, node:crypto) against the exact canonical payload.
