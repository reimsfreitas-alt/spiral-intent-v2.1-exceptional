THE AUTHORITY MODEL

Four keys. No overlap.

"No single authority can authorize, execute, and verify the same action."

Policy Authority

Signs what is authorized — action, target, limits — before anything runs.

Execution Authority

The only holder of write access. Refuses to act without a valid signed authorization.

Verification Authority

Reads the external system independently. Never asks the executor what happened.

Receipt Authority

Signs the outcome — only after an independent observation exists.
