THE MISSING LAYER

A 200 response proves a call. It does not prove the intended effect.

Modern agents, workflows and automation systems can trigger consequential actions across payment, ERP, infrastructure and operational systems. Their own logs are still claims made by the system that performed the action.

The system that acts should not be the system that attests that it worked.
THE MECHANISM

Seven stages. One answer.

Spiral separates the authorities that declare, authorize, execute, observe and attest. The final verdict is derived from evidence, not from the worker's success state.

THE DIFFERENCE

Execution success is not effect confirmation.

Spiral does not replace IAM, an ERP, an orchestrator or ordinary observability. It answers a narrower question that becomes critical when software can act on your behalf.

WHAT YOUR EXECUTOR CAN SAY

Request accepted. Call completed. Status code returned. Operation identifier recorded. Local state updated.

WHAT SPIRAL ADDS

What was authorized, what was claimed, what the external system actually reported, whether those states correspond, and a signed verdict preserving the result.

NON-COLLUSION BY DESIGN

Worker ≠ Observer ≠ Verifier.

The product is not the diagram. The separation of authority is. Each component gets the minimum authority required to perform its role.

VERDICTS

No invented certainty.

Spiral distinguishes successful confirmation from deviation and from insufficient evidence. A technical observation failure does not get silently promoted to success.

CONFIRMED

The observed effect corresponds to what was authorized.

DEVIATED

The observed effect does not correspond to the authorization.

INCONCLUSIVE

The evidence is insufficient to establish either state. This is uncertainty represented honestly, not hidden by a green dashboard.
