Do not measure what the agent says. Measure what the business actually received.

Spiral Intent is designed to sit beside systems that act. It independently compares authorized intent, executed activity and observed external state — turning deviations into evidence that operations, finance, risk and leadership can act on.

WHERE VALUE APPEARS

One control pattern. Multiple economic consequences.

The same verification mechanism can be applied wherever an automated action changes money, work, records, commitments or operational state.

FINANCE

Compare authorized amounts, payments, credits, reconciliations, approvals and observed ledger state. Surface overpayment, underpayment, duplication, unauthorized change or unexplained variance as evidence for investigation.

OPERATIONS

Verify that work orders, inventory movements, deliveries, service changes, workflow transitions and system updates actually produced the state the organization intended.

PEOPLE & HR

Where software agents act on HR workflows, verify that authorized changes to employee records, approvals, onboarding, scheduling or access states correspond to the intended action.

PROCUREMENT

Check whether sourcing, purchase, vendor and approval actions produced the commercial state that was authorized — especially when agents negotiate or execute across systems.

REVENUE

Use independent observation to investigate missed transactions, incorrect adjustments, failed handoffs, pricing deviations or workflow latency that can suppress realized revenue.

GENERAL CONTROL

Build a verifiable record for any consequential automated process where “SUCCESS” from the executor is not sufficient evidence that the business outcome occurred.

OPERATING MODEL

From inspection to continuous improvement.

01 · OBSERVE

Capture the event chain without granting the verifier control over execution.

02 · CLASSIFY

Return deterministic states such as CONFIRMED, DEVIATED or INCONCLUSIVE rather than forcing certainty.

03 · QUANTIFY

Attach business magnitude: value at risk, loss, delay, rework, leakage or avoided exposure.

04 · EXPLAIN

Trace the evidence back to the authorized action and observed external state.

05 · IMPROVE

Use repeated deviations to identify weak controls, process friction, poor data or unsafe autonomy boundaries.

06 · SCALE

Apply the same assurance pattern across finance, HR, procurement, operations and other consequential workflows.
