Spiral Intent · Security posture

What exists. What the architecture supports. What remains roadmap.

This page is deliberately conservative: implementation claims are separated from architectural support and future work.

Implemented

  • Signed authorization envelope (Ed25519)
  • Least-privilege separation: write ≠ read ≠ sign
  • Independent read-only observer
  • Replay resistance with nonce and idempotency
  • Append-only, hash-chained ledger
  • Deterministic verdict
  • Job lease with fencing

Architecture supports

  • New external systems through connectors
  • Webhook observation alongside polling

Roadmap

  • Platform authentication
  • Key rotation / external KMS
  • Multi-tenant isolation
  • Human approval gate wiring

Trust boundary

The executor is not the authority that signs the receipt and does not receive the read-only credentials used for independent observation. Verification compares authorization with observed external state.

Threat-model discipline

A signature proves provenance and integrity of the signed payload; it does not prove that the external system is honest. A hash chain detects retroactive modification of linked records; it does not by itself protect the entire ledger host from destruction. Insufficient observation remains INCONCLUSIVE rather than being promoted to CONFIRMED by assumption.
