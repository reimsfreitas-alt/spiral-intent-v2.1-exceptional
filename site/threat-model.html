SPIRAL INTENT / THREAT MODEL

Assume a component can be compromised.

The design limits what a compromised component can independently prove about its own work.

ComponentAuthorityBoundary
Workerwritecannot observe or sign
Observerreadcannot write
Verifierverdictdoes not execute
Policyauthorizationdoes not execute
Ledgerappend recordhash-chain integrity

A valid receipt proves cryptographic integrity of the receipt. It does not magically prove that a deployment's observer credential was independent; that remains an operational control.
